RIKEN QEC Workshop

15 to 18 February 2027

RIKEN iTHEMS, Wako, Japan

Four days on quantum error correction for physicists from high-energy theory, condensed matter, and quantum information. The first two days open with tutorials that start from the basics. Invited talks then cover recent work, from fault-tolerant architectures to codes built from gauge theories and holography.

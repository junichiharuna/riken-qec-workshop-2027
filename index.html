RIKEN iTHEMS Workshop on Quantum Error Correction

15 to 18 February 2027

RIKEN iTHEMS, Wako, Japan

Quantum error correction bridges mathematics, condensed matter physics, high-energy theory, and quantum gravity. This workshop brings researchers from these fields together to share what is understood and what the open problems are. Tutorials on the first two days start from the basics, and invited talks on recent work follow.

RIKEN QEC Workshop

15 to 18 February 2027

RIKEN iTHEMS, Wako, Japan

Quantum error correction now reaches into mathematics, condensed matter physics, high-energy theory, and quantum gravity, and the overall picture is hard to see from any one of these fields. This workshop brings researchers from all of them together to share what is understood, what the open problems are, and where the subject should go next. Tutorials on the first two days start from the basics, so that participants from outside quantum information have a common footing for the invited talks. The meeting is kept small to leave time for discussion, and we hope it becomes a starting point for work across these fields.
